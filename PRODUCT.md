# Product

## Register

product

## Users

InfoEducație competitors, teachers, organizers, jury members, alumni, and
public visitors. For the robotics testing surface, five teams coordinate access
to one physical drone arena while competitors and spectators follow the live
state from phones, laptops, and a shared display in a busy event space.

## Product Purpose

InfoEducație supports participation in Romania's national digital creation
competition and makes its public activity transparent. The robotics testing
surface must give every team an understandable, fair way to use a scarce test
environment while making the active turn, queue, and remaining time obvious to
everyone.

## Brand Personality

Youthful, optimistic, and direct. The experience should feel energetic and
welcoming without becoming noisy, childish, or difficult to scan under time
pressure.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic dark operations dashboards, corporate SaaS card walls,
glassmorphism, decorative gradients, game-like competitive rankings, and
interfaces that hide the shared-resource rules behind clever terminology.
Do not replace the established InfoEducație identity with a new visual system.

## Design Principles

1. Make the shared arena state unmistakable at a glance.
2. Explain fairness through visible queue and time information.
3. Separate public transparency from authenticated team actions.
4. Preserve the recognizable InfoEducație voice and visual identity.
5. Design for stressful, mobile, and shared-screen use before decorative polish.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All controls must work with keyboard and assistive
technology, status cannot rely on color alone, countdown updates must not flood
live regions, touch targets must be at least 44 pixels, and layouts must remain
usable at 320 pixels wide and with reduced motion.
